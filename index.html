About Us

Empowering Communities, Shaping Futures

RD Shikshan Evam Samaj Kalyan Samiti, established in 2012 by Shri Manoj Bhavsar in Bhopal, is a non-profit organization committed to education, skill development, women empowerment, and community welfare. We work to create opportunities, build confidence, and empower individuals—especially women and youth—to become self-reliant and active contributors to society.

Through our grassroots initiatives, we strive to reach underserved communities and create sustainable, long-term impact. We believe that education and empowerment are the foundation of a stronger and more inclusive society.

Our mission is simple: Empower people, strengthen communities, and create a better future for all.

Our Programs

Structured activities designed to create meaningful opportunities.

Educational Support

Empowering Children Through Quality Education

Millions of children lack access to basic education due to financial hardship and systemic barriers. Our Child Education initiative works directly in underserved communities to ensure every child gets the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive in a safe environment.

Key Initiatives:

After-School Learning Centers Providing remedial coaching, homework help, and foundational literacy/numeracy for first-generation learners.
School Supplies & Scholarships Distributing free books, uniforms, stationery, and financial assistance to prevent school dropouts.
Digital Literacy Equipping classrooms with basic computers and interactive learning tools to bridge the digital divide.
Holistic Development Conducting extracurricular workshops, sports activities, and life-skills training.

Women Empowerment

Fostering Independence and Leadership

We actively support the journey of women towards self-reliance, providing essential tools and creating environments where they can build confidence, grow economically, and lead their communities.

Key Initiatives:

Skill Development & Vocational TrainingProviding women with practical skills to improve employment and income opportunities.
Entrepreneurship SupportEncouraging women to start and grow small businesses and self-employment ventures.
Financial IndependencePromoting financial literacy, savings, and sustainable income generation.
Digital LiteracyHelping women develop essential digital and technology skills.
Education & AwarenessSupporting girls and women through education, awareness programs, and career guidance.
Self-Help Groups (SHGs)Promoting collective action, savings, and income-generating activities.
Leadership DevelopmentBuilding confidence and encouraging women to participate in community decision-making.
Legal & Social AwarenessCreating awareness about women’s rights, safety, equality, and available support systems.

Youth Development

Guiding the Next Generation of Leaders

Empowering young individuals by providing the guidance, skills, and confidence they need to transition successfully into adulthood, find meaningful careers, and become responsible citizens.

Key Focus Areas:

Skill Development & EmployabilityOffering practical training programs to prepare youth for the modern workforce and self-reliance.
Career AwarenessProviding counseling and guidance to help youth navigate educational pathways and career options.
Digital & Communication SkillsEnsuring young people are fluent in essential digital tools and effective communication techniques.
Leadership & Confidence BuildingCultivating self-assurance and leadership capabilities to help youth overcome challenges.
Education SupportAssisting with continued education and mitigating barriers to learning.
Constructive Community ParticipationEncouraging active, positive engagement in local civic initiatives and social development.
